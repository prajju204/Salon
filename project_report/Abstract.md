# ABSTRACT

The beauty and grooming industry has seen significant digital transformation, yet many premium salons still struggle with fragmented systems for booking appointments, managing staff schedules, and selling grooming products. Luxe Groom is a comprehensive, centralized platform that seamlessly integrates premium salon service booking with an e-commerce storefront for grooming products. The system is designed to provide an intuitive and luxurious digital experience for customers while offering robust administrative and operational tools for salon management. 

Luxe Groom addresses the limitations of traditional booking methods by introducing a Progressive Web Application (PWA) equipped with real-time scheduling, secure payment gateways, and role-based access control. The platform caters to four distinct user roles: Customers, Admin, Staff (Stylists/Barbers), and Delivery Personnel. Customers can browse services, book appointments, purchase products, and track orders. Staff members have dedicated dashboards to manage their appointments, update their availability, and track performance metrics. Delivery personnel can efficiently handle and update the status of product orders. The Admin module serves as the central control hub, providing comprehensive oversight of all operations, financial reporting, and user management.

Developed using modern web technologies including React (Vite), Node.js, Express, and MongoDB, Luxe Groom ensures high performance, scalability, and security. The system incorporates advanced features such as real-time push notifications, a digital wallet for loyalty programs, and automated review management. By bridging the gap between service provision and product retail, Luxe Groom not only enhances customer satisfaction but also optimizes operational efficiency, paving the way for a modernized approach to salon business management.

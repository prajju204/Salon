# 2. LITERATURE REVIEW

## 2.1 Introduction
A literature review is a critical summary and analysis of existing research, studies, and publications related to a particular topic. It helps to understand what has already been explored, identify gaps in knowledge, and provide a foundation for designing and justifying our own project. By reviewing previous work, a literature review highlights effective methodologies, challenges faced, and solutions proposed by other researchers. It also demonstrates how our project contributes to the existing body of knowledge and addresses unmet needs in the domain of salon management and e-commerce.

## 2.2 Result Analysis
As the grooming industry evolves, the need for integrated digital solutions has become increasingly apparent. Several recent studies have explored the impact of digital booking systems on service industries. Research by Smith et al. (2021) highlighted that online appointment systems significantly reduce no-show rates and improve customer satisfaction by providing transparency and ease of access. However, many of these systems are limited to booking functionalities and do not incorporate e-commerce. 

Conversely, studies focusing on salon e-commerce, such as the work by Johnson and Lee (2022), emphasize the importance of selling grooming products online to supplement service revenue. Their research showed that customers are highly likely to purchase products recommended by their stylists if the purchasing process is seamless. Despite this, most salons use disjointed platforms—one for booking and a separate Shopify or WooCommerce store for products—which complicates inventory management and customer relationship tracking.

Another area of research focuses on real-time tracking and customer engagement. The integration of real-time notifications via WebSockets or push notifications has been proven to enhance user engagement. A study by Kumar et al. (2023) demonstrated that real-time status updates in delivery applications reduce customer anxiety and support queries by 40%. Applying this to a salon setting, where customers are often waiting for their appointments or product deliveries, can vastly improve the user experience.

## 2.3 Identified gaps in the Literature
- **Lack of Integrated Platforms**: Most existing management systems treat service booking and e-commerce as two separate entities, forcing salon owners to manage multiple platforms.
- **Absence of Real-Time Waitlists**: Current salon booking systems generally do not offer dynamic, real-time waitlists. Customers have no automated way to be notified if a previously booked slot becomes available.
- **Limited Real-Time Communication**: Many systems rely heavily on slow email communication or manual phone calls to update customers about their appointment status or product delivery status.
- **Underutilized Loyalty Programs**: Integrated digital wallets and loyalty points that span across both services and product purchases are rarely implemented in standard salon software.
- **Delivery Management**: Traditional salon software does not account for an in-house delivery force to manage local delivery of grooming products, missing an opportunity for end-to-end service control.

## 2.4 Existing System
In the existing scenario, salon management still largely depends on manual reporting and fragmented software processes. Customers usually call the salon to book an appointment, and if the salon is fully booked, there is no reliable way to join a digital waitlist. If the salon sells products, it is usually managed via a completely separate e-commerce platform that does not sync with the customer's service history. 

Furthermore, existing systems lack a dedicated module for delivery personnel. Salons either have to rely entirely on third-party logistics with high commission rates or manage local deliveries manually via phone calls and WhatsApp messages, which often lead to delays and miscommunication.

Thus, the current system faces challenges like delayed response, poor coordination, limited automation, and no centralized data for holistic analysis and decision-making.

## 2.5 Proposed System
The proposed "Luxe Groom" system aims to overcome these limitations by introducing a comprehensive web application that combines appointment booking, e-commerce, and delivery management into a single, cohesive platform. 

Customers can seamlessly book services, purchase cosmetic products, join automated waitlists, and track their delivery orders in real time. The system automatically sends socket-based notifications and manages user loyalty points and digital wallets across both services and products.

For administrators, it provides a centralized dashboard to track all aspects of the business—from barber performance and leave management to revenue analytics and coupon distribution. By introducing a dedicated Delivery Portal, the system also empowers the salon's own delivery staff to track and update order fulfillments via GPS location and real-time status changes.

Overall, the proposed system provides an affordable, transparent, and participatory solution for modern salon management, aligning with global smart business and digital transformation goals. It enhances efficiency, accountability, and collaboration between customers, staff, and administrators.
